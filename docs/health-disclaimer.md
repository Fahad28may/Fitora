# Health Disclaimer

> Draft — requires professional legal review before production use.

Fitora provides general fitness and nutrition information and estimates. It is **not** medical advice, a medical device, or a substitute for a qualified healthcare professional.

- Calorie, macro, and BMR/TDEE figures shown in Fitora are **estimates** based on standard formulas and the information you provide. They are not medically exact and may not be appropriate for every individual.
- Fitora's AI coach does not diagnose conditions, prescribe or recommend changes to medication, or provide individualized medical treatment instructions.
- If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, are under 18, or are considering a significant change to your diet or exercise routine, consult a qualified healthcare professional before acting on information from this app.
- If a goal you set appears potentially unsafe (e.g. an aggressive rate of weight loss/gain), Fitora will flag this and suggest safer alternatives rather than silently supporting it — this is a safety heuristic, not a medical assessment.
- If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately. Fitora is not equipped to provide emergency care.

This disclaimer is surfaced during onboarding, alongside the AI coach, within nutrition features, and in relevant settings/help sections — not hidden solely inside the Terms of Service.
